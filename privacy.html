Privacy Policy

Color By Number: Peaceful Mind · Last updated: November 2026

Your progress stays on your device

Peaceful Mind has no accounts. Your coloring progress and settings are saved only on your iPhone and are never uploaded to us. Deleting the app removes them.

Ads

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: a short ad when a picture starts, and optional videos you can choose to watch for a hint, an extra picture or to unlock a level. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device type, approximate location from your IP address and how you interact with ads. Google's own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy) describes how it uses this information.

Your choices

Where the law requires it (for example in the EU and UK), you'll be asked for consent before any personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings › Privacy choices. Peaceful Mind does not use Apple's advertising identifier and does not track you across other companies' apps.

Photos

When you tap Save, the app asks for permission to add your finished picture to your photo library. It can only add images. It cannot see or read your existing photos.

Sharing

When you tap Share, your picture is handed to the standard iOS share sheet. Where it goes next is your choice, and is covered by that app's own privacy policy.

Children

Peaceful Mind is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will appear on this page and in the app.
