Frequently asked questions

How do I play?
Pick a theme and a picture, choose a color at the bottom of the screen, then tap the areas with the matching number. Pinch to zoom in on small areas.

How do I find the last few areas?
Tap the hint button. After a short optional video, the remaining areas for the selected color are highlighted.

Why can I only color 3 pictures a day?
The app is free. You get 3 new pictures every day, and you can watch a short video for an extra picture at any time. Reopening a picture you've already opened today, or viewing a finished one, is always free.

How do I unlock a level early?
Levels unlock as you finish pictures. You can also unlock any level right away by watching a short video.

Will I lose my progress?
Progress is saved on your iPhone automatically. It is removed only if you delete the app.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?
Open Settings in the app (the gear on the Themes screen) and tap Privacy choices. This option appears in regions where it's required.
